How to play
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your base.
  • Move tokens clockwise round the board, then up your coloured lane to the centre.
  • Land on an opponent to send them back to base — except on stars and start squares, which are safe.
  • A 6, a capture, or getting a token home earns another roll. Three 6s in a row and you lose your turn.
  • You need the exact number to reach the centre. Get all four tokens home to win.
  • Keyboard: Space rolls the dice.
